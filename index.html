Style direction

Warm, calm and a little regal

This brings together the four looks you asked for: lux organic, sleek monochrome, warm and cozy, and modern royale. One base runs through every room, so the house feels like a single home rather than separate rooms.

One warm base

Ivory walls, greige and taupe fabrics, and oak and walnut wood in every room. Natural textures do the work: linen, bouclé, travertine-look stone.

Black and brass for edges

Matte black on frames, chair legs and the TV wall. Brushed brass on lamps and pendants. This is where the sleek, monochrome look comes from.

One olive accent per room

Olive velvet on a pillow, a chair or a print, and only once per room. That restraint is what keeps the royal touch elegant.

Walkable by rule: every main path stays at least 3 feet wide, and nothing is bought just to fill a corner. Each fit check below gives the actual clearance.

Before buying

Check these at your walkthrough

  • Office window. The builder plans show no window in the office (the den). The listing calls it a bedroom, which normally needs a window. Confirm what was actually built.
  • Fireplace location. The plans show one electric fireplace, in the main-floor great room. The listing describes a fireplace in the bonus room. Check which rooms have one.
  • TV wall. Measure the space above the fireplace, and ask whether there is wood blocking and a power outlet behind it for the mount.
  • Island overhang. The plans show a 12-inch eating bar. Measure it and take the stool seat height (24–26 inches) when you shop.
  • Bedroom 3 closet door. Check which way it swings before choosing nightstands.
  • Delivery path. The stairs are 3'-6" wide with a landing, and the front door is 42 inches. Check large items against these before ordering.
